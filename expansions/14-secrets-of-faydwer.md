### **The Beginning**

- Secrets of Faydwer was announced on July 11, 2007, and was launched on November 13, 2007.
- The initial announcement may be found [here](https://www.everquest.com/news/imported-eq-enus-50433) and the expansion-release patch message may be found [here](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/sdetail.html?story=11214).

### **What's Going On**

- Meldrath the Malignant, the evil gnomish necromancer, has begun gathering his armies in the Steamfont Mountains, preparing to invade Ak'anon.
- Meldrath has launched Mechanotus, a floating fortress. In the process, he tore the very fabric of the continent of Faydwer, and revealing previously unexplored ancient lands.

### **New Features**

- [Heroic Statistics](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:Heroic_Stats) will be found on many SOF-dropped items, both at the raid and group level, and will modify things such as avoidance and mitigation, give extra melee damage, add extra modifiers to HP, Mana, and Endurance, and so forth.
- The level cap increases from 75 to 80.
- Fifteen new zones have launched.
- New alternate advancement abilities have been put in place, approximately 240-250 AA worth for each class.

### **Gear in Secrets of Faydwer**

- There are four new tiers of groupable gear, along with four new tiers of raid gear. Tier 4 group gear has approximately 550 HP and is infusible, tier 4 raid gear has approximately 850 HP and is infusible.
- Each tier of gear, in addition to having full sets of visible armor, will include a full range of weapon types.
- Group and Raid Armor progression is listed **[this Armor Guide](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:Secrets_of_Faydwer_Armor_Guide)**
- As we get more info, further details on both group and raid gear will be found on their respective tier pages, linked below.
- Also refer: [SOFG Tier Four](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:SOFG_Tier_Four)

### **Spells in Secrets of Faydwer**

- Spells will be structured in a three rank system in SOF, similar to the one found in TSS and TBS.
- Rk. I spells and tomes are bought from vendors:

  - Level 76 from [Ellymil Cogswin](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=26794) in the Dragonscale Hills at approximate location +900, -535.
  - Level 77 from [Kedem Teague](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=26795) in the Loping Plains at approximate location -2320, -520.
  - Level 78 from [Inizen Nogglezop](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=26796) in Fortress Mechanotus at approximate location +890, -1455, +405.
  - Level 79 from [Naene Ebonlocke](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=26797) in the Hills of Shade at approximate location -65, +1475, 0.
  - Level 80 from [Seyan Ebonlocke](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=26799) in the Hills of Shade at approximate location +980, -580.

- Rk. II spells and tomes are dropped by level-appropriate mobs or bought off of vendors requiring faction. The faction spells are detailed on the individual class pages, found through [Classes](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:Classes).
- [Rk. III spells and tomes](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/quest.html?quest=4404) result from turnins of runes from raid events or are purchased off of elite vendors after unlocking via a combination of faction and group and/or raid missions.

### **SOF Zones**

- There are fifteen new zones with SOF, twelve primarily group zones and three primarily raid zones.
- You can download maps of all of the new zones [here.](http://www.obsservers.com/eq.htm)
- Group content in SOF is separated in to four tiers:

- **Tier one** consists of [Dragonscale Hills](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=539) and the [Loping Plains](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=529). More info on T1, including details on loot and what to expect from the zones in general can be found [here](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:SOFG_Tier_One).
- **Tier two** consists of seven zones; [Bloodmoon Keep](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=530), The Mechamatic Guardian(Instance), [The Hills of Shade](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=531), [The Crypt of Shade](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=532)(Instance), [The Fortress Mechanotus](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=533), and the [Gyrospires Beza](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=537) and [Zeka](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=538). More info on T2, including details on loot and what to expect from the zones in general can be found [here](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:SOFG_Tier_Two).
- **Tier three** consists of three zones; the [S.H.I.P. Workshop](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=536), [The Steam Factory](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=534), and [Meldrath's Majestic Mansion](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=535). More info on T3, including details on loot and what to expect from the zones in general can be found [here](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:SOFG_Tier_Three).
- **Tier four** consists of a single zone, [Crystallos, the Lair of the Awakened](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=540). Information on T4 loot and the group version of Crystallos itself, may be found [here](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/EQ:SOFG_Tier_Four).

There will be 28 raid events in SOF, structured in to four tiers. There aren't very many details out yet, so the pages below will mostly be placeholders for now.

- It's currently unknown what raid T1 will consist of; as info becomes available, it will be found [here](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki.html?e=EQ:SOFR_Tier_One).

If you have a Mechamagical Metamorpher [item=76280], you can downgrade a rune by combining it inside the metamorpher: Glowing Prismatic Rune to Whirring Clockwork Rune, Whirring Clockwork Rune to Humming Finsternacht Rune.
Upon handing in a Humming Finsternacht Rune or Whirring Clockwork Rune:
Kalim'Kar the Gold breaks into a wide, foolishly elated grin, 'You've found exactly what I need! For your efforts, and by the command of Keikolin, I grant you magic of tremendous power. Use it wisely.'
_Additionally, on handing in your final rune of any type..._
Kalim`Kar tells you, 'There are no further magics I can teach you from this type of rune.'
