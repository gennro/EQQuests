## **Introduction**

Shattering of Ro is EverQuest's 32nd expansion. It will launch on December 2, 2025.

* * *

## **Content & Features**

- 6 Expansion Zones
- New Raids, Quests, and Missions
- New Spells, Combat Abilities, and AAs
- New Collections
- New race and class combination: Vah Shir Druid
- New Weapon Ornaments
- Activated Items Key Ring - A new key ring that accepts most nonexpendable items with activatable effects that are not already part of another key ring.

## **Getting There**

You can get to Shattering of Ro zones through several ways.

- Via the Guild Hall portal NPC [Zeflmin Werlikanin](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=17516). Purchase Charred Rune-Etrched Stone from him and give it to him to be ported to Scarred Grove at /way -656, -876, -23.
- You can get to Scarred Grove through Wakening Lands by clicking the burning tree at /way 507, -1042, -20.
- Druid and Wizard ports will take you to Scarred Grove as well.

## **List of Tasks**

Listed here are all tasks and group missions. Group missions have a ? hour lockout to request a different mission.

- **Bolded** tasks represent progression task arc.

  - Each zones adventures and tasks make up a linear series to be completed in the order they're listed below.

**Note: Most or all starting task NPCs are findable with the CTRL+F (Find) option. However some of these NPC's are located in other zones besides where the quest takes place.**

Shattering of Ro quests have 2 factions. Verdant Guardians and The Burning Host's.
Your starting faction is based upon your race, class, and deity.
Good races are Verdant Guardians and evil races are The Burning Host's.
It looks like Scarred Grove is the only zone that has this faction system at the moment.
