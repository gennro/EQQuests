### **Introduction**

**The Broken Mirror** is EverQuest's 22nd expansion. It was launched on Wednesday, November 18, 2015

* * *

### **Content & Features**

- Level Scaling Raids - Instanced versions of Plane of Hate and Plane of Fear that scale for level 75-105 raids.
- 7 Expansion Zones - 4 completely new zones and 3 revamped zones.
- Illusion Key Ring - Access your illusions in one easy location!
- New Quests, Heroic Adventures, Missions, and Additional Raids
- New Spells and AAs

## **Getting There**

- The entry zone here is the [Plane of Health](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zones.html?zstrat=1057).
- Entered through the [Plane of Tranquility](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=159) by touching a floating octahedron between the buildings on the center of the island, path available via the Find command.

- If you bought/buy the Collector's Edition of the expansion, you can claim an item Contract of the Chosen mercenary.
- If you bought/buy the Premium Edition of the expansion, you can claim an item Shroud of Relife illusion

- [The Broken Mirror map pack info](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/forum.html?forum=1&mid=1447444796254447640)

### **Dichotomic spells**

**How you get them**

You can buy your Dichotomic spells from [Warlowe Rida](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=50305) in the Plane of Tranquility. She is located in a room next to the portal to Plane of Nightmare.

Progressive spells use special mechanics that put 'hidden' spells on your character at level 250, and it's those spells that get triggered when you use the base ability.

A script on the base spell item gives you these hidden spells as well as the base spell. If you just get them from /spellscribe, the hidden spells won't get applied and the base spell won't have anything to trigger.

The base spell autocasts a second spell that scales up based on your The Broken Mirror Progression. Each class also gets a 6 Rank spell line. Example is this [Dichotomic Fire](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/item.html?item=124906) is for the Wizard class and will cast [Dichotomic Fire 1](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/spell.html?spell=49224)

Note that the secondary spells are level 250 which mean that certain mods will not work on them, notably worn focus, spell focus (bard/kerafyrm familiar), the new vortex effect, and a couple others that have level caps on them.

The base spell has 1 point of damage on it so that it can be focused by spell haste, twincast, and a few other things. The extra damage you see is from passive AAs, spell damage, focus, etc which make that 1 point go to 1k.

**How you make them better**

The spells are tied to your progression through the expansion. You'll be able to cast at least the first rank just for buying it, but getting the best rank will require raiding.

Each rank of these spells is tied to an achievement, and earning that achievement will grant you an item to use to upgrade your spells.

## Overview

There are three open zones, two locked zones and two raid zones in The Broken Mirror Progression. This article deals with the non raid zones.

Progression is strictly linear, with a storyline that consist of the first three Mercenary Tasks. Upon completion you receive a Mirror Fragment of Anashti Sul which when clicked transports you between the two Sul Vius Demiplanes.

Heroic adventures and mercenary tasks can be completed any time, but to request them, you'll need to have completed all prior adventures or tasks for that zone. This applies to zones in Tier 1a and 1b. For Tier 1c please see the note accompanying the list for Tier 1c.

- Tier 1a:

  - [Plane of Health](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=1057)
  - [Sul Vius: Demiplane of Life](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=1058)

- Tier 1b: (locked behind Tier 1a progression tasks)

  - [Sul Vius: Demiplane of Decay](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=1059)
  - [Crypt of Decay (Ruins of Lxanvom)](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=1061)

- Tier 1c:

  - [Crypt of Sul](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/zone.html?zstrat=1060)

---

**The Phases**
The Starting Phase
From 100% - 75% it will be standard fight. Anashti hits hard and fast. Averaging about 30k a hit, she will strike through defenses regularly. It is recommended to have someone that can slow her. Around 95% the bokons will spawn in the southern tunnel.
The Gifts Phase
Around 75% - Anashti Sul will enter her 'Gifts' phase in which she will put two different gifts on a random target. She will either use Gifts of Endless Life which is a heal over time, or Gifts of Living Death which is a damage over time. There is a special achievement for not curing either of these.
The Porting Phase
Around 56% - Anashti Sul will port herself downstairs with the main tank. It is recommend that you have your party start moving down to the bottom around 57% because there is a likelihood that some may be afflicted with enfeeblement which has a snare debuff attached to it. When she does her port, you have 60 seconds to do as much DPS as you can before she ports back upstairs. She will port up and down every 60 seconds.
The Withering Phase
The final and most frustrating phase is the Withering Phase. At 36% she will start her withering curse cycle on the entire group. There are three different curses; Withering Limbs XIII which affects all DPS based classes negatively, Withering Faith XIII which affects all healers negatively, including Shadowknights attempting to do lifetap abilities, and Withering Physicality XIII which is a nightmare for tanks. Unless you are a healer, it is ideal for DPS & tanks to have the Withering Faith effect - as it will be less crippling. It is absolutely not recommended to do this without a real player healer, because if you use a mercenary healer - he/she will continually cast a curse cure which will potentially cycle you into a curse you do not want to have for your specific class. Each curse respectfully lasts for about 2 minutes. It is recommended that tanks use any form of deflection about 3 seconds before the curse fades due to the likelihood you may fade into the physicality curse. Using a deflection type ability if hit with physicality will give you enough time to use a potion or get a curse cure to cycle you into a more favorable curse.
**_NOTE: She will continue to port every 60 seconds up and down during the Withering Phase. If you are unable to defeat her by the time she ports a 3rd time, there is a chance you likely will not win._**
The Bokons & Ooze
It is good to have a Knight class off tank the bokons and oozies. The bokons spawn in the southern tunnel around 95% and make their way to fight. It is recommended to keep a player in opening to that tunnel to intercept while keeping an eye out for the oozies. The oozies spawn at the lower level, but due to pathing issues, they rarely actually work their way up the tunnel - so they simply just "port" into the fight. The bokons only hit for about 3-4k, but the oozes will hit for about 20k and backstab. This is not a good thing if a ooze gets on your main tank. Just kite the bokons into the oozes and they will die instantly. Be advised bokons will regularly use the Enfeeblement spell, which does both a snare and halves the target's cast timer (very bad for healers).
**_NOTE: It is recommended that your main healer puts down healing wards because the bokons will cast use the enfeeblement spell; which not only has a snare effect but it slows your cast timers down as well. This can spell trouble for the healer attempting to throw out quick heals to the tank._**
Failure Mechanics
There are three mechanics that will lead to instant death.
**1\. Tank swapping.** If you attempt to swap out a tank for a pet, or another tank and that main tank is still on her most hated list, she will start a mechanic called _'Internal Rot'_ that will do DoT damage between 80-100k a tick! The only way to counteract this is to get back within range of her to fight.
**2\. 20 Minute Timer Fail.** You have 20 minutes to beat her. She even tells you this. If you do not stop her in under 20 minutes, the event fails and you will get death touched.
**3\. Withering Physicality.** If you are unfortunate to have this effect and do not get a cure in time, you will end up getting one-shot by either Anashti Sul, the bokons, or the oozes. This effect is not an effect anyone should have on at any time.
**4\. Disease or Corruption.** Either one of these types of spells or procs will power Anashti Sul up. She will recover 5% of her overall health and hit a bit harder.

**Task Summary**
_Upon acquiring the mission from Lashun Novashine, you will be selecting the 'Adventure' instance for Crypt of Sul. ---

**Engaging Anashti Sul**
_It is absolutely not recommended to attempt this mission unless you have a full group with a real player healer. Anashti Sul hits like a truck. She averages for about 40k, is very fast, and will strike through defenses very regularly - so make certain you have a competent tank! You are going to need someone to slow her or at least use an item that will proc a slow to help mitigate her onslaught of attacks. There are four phases to this fight, in which Anashti Sul will shift forms three different times._

---

**The Phases**
**Phase 1: Lady of Life**
_>\> The first phase, you will encounter Anashti Sul, the Lady of Life. At 90% she will cast mark on herself that needs to be dispelled. There will be four health crystals that will spawn that will rapidly regenerate her health. You need to destroy these to make progress. In addition, you will have 3 skeleton adds that will pop approximately every 90 seconds. These adds hit for an average of 20k, so topping that with Anashti Sul attacking the tank - it is recommended that the tank uses some form of deflection. This is mostly a DPS race. You want to do a solid burn until you get her down to 70%. When she hits 70%, she will shift into the next phase._
**Phase 2: Damsel of Decay**
_>\> This is the phase that most groups commonly wipe on. She evolves into Anashti Sul, the Damsel of Decay and hits like a truck. There are two general mechanics to this phase. The poison blast and the mortal decay. Both are targeted AEs that Anashti will perform frequently. The trick is to move your group into one of the three blue auras that will be illuminated in the room. Do as much DPS as you can to her before she breaks off, and runs crazy around the room before running to the center and casts poison blast. Keep this up until you get her down to 50% for her next phase._
**Phase 3: Mini-Bosses**
_>\> Each mini-boss will have a protective shield up until you kite them across a specific aura. The first mini-boss you will want to kite to the north blue aura, the second mini-boss you will want to kite to the east blue aura, and the final mini-boss you will want to kite to the west aura. When you successfully kite the mobs through the aura, you will get a message alerting you that the mob is now vulnerable to attack. Each of these mini-bosses are no harder than the zone trash mobs, hitting for an average of 25k tops. When you have completed this step, you will enter the final phase._
**Phase 4: Enslaver of Souls**
_>\> You will want your group to remain within the west aura which should generate a purple hue. Anashti Sul at this point will be known as the Enslaver of Souls and will run towards the center again. It is imperative that you have your tank drag her toward that west aura to protect your group from being hit by her poison blast. Finish her off for the win._

---

**The Auras & Trap Mechanics**
_The number one thing that leads to the demise of most groups attempting this is the trap mechanics which are targeted AEs. The first is a mortal decay AE which does not do that much damage, however the poison blast AE is fatal. Ideally, you want to keep your group bunched up together at all times. Under no circumstance should anyone split up. The reason for this is because the poison blast mechanic is a mega DD towards the targeted player. If that player runs off to hide, that person will receive the full damage from the poison blast; however, if they stay with their group, the amount of damage done will simply be divided among all the players._
Example 1: 150k DD when player is by themselves will be hit for 150k.
Example 2: 150k DD when the player is standing with his/her 5 other buddies will hit everyone for 25k.
_As you can see, it is mathematically more beneficial for everyone to take the hit and receive a splash heal than run off and die. Splash heals are going to be essential during this event considering the trap mechanics are going to be a constant nuisance to deal with. During that second phase, you will see a blue aura pop up in one of 3 locations. It is recommended you have 1 person on 'aura lookout' and call out in chat or voice the location of where the aura pops, because you only have 5 seconds to get to safety. Be sure you have levitation disabled or you will not receive the benefits of the aura protection. You will actually hover right outside of it._
**_NOTE: There is a likelihood that you and your group may end up afflicted with Poison Blast or Mortal Decay before the auras become visible or available. If this happens, keep your group close together at all times to lessen the damage that is done and simply heal splash your way through it._**

---

**Mega DPS Strategies**
**Skip Phase 1**
_>\> It is recommended in any attempt at this, that everyone have enough DPS to be able to burn her down starting at 90% and quickly get her to 70% **before** the health crystals spawn. Doing this will prevent the headache of trying to split your DPS off to destroy the crystals and watch her recover all her health back, additionally it will save you from being overrun with skeleton adds._
**Skip Phase 1 & 2**
_>\> If you have the proper group makeup with synergy, you can actually dish out enough DPS to circumvent the first two phases and go straight to Phase 3. This requires **A LOT** of DPS; however, has been the most preferred method by most players to getting the event accomplished._
Example: Shadowknight, Cleric, Wizard, Wizard, Enchanter, Berserker
_There are many other group makeups out there that you can create for solid DPS. This is not an easy event, so don't hesitate to experiment!_

---

**Failure Mechanics**
_The only failure mechanic this event has is if you use any corruption or disease based attacks on Anashti Sul when she evolves from Lady of Life to Decay. When she is in Decay & Enslaver form, any corruption or disease based attacks will power her up. She will recover approximately 5% of her overall health and will start striking harder._
