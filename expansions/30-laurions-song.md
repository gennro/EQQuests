## **Introduction**

Laurion's Song is EverQuest's 30th expansion. It launched on December 5th, 2023.

* * *

## **Content & Features**

- 7 Expansion Zones
- New Raids, Quests, and Missions
- New Spells, Combat Abilities, and AAs
- New Collections
- Alternate Personas - Swap to another class while retaining your name, inventory, bank, crafting skills, keyring and more!
- New Weapon Ornaments
- Level cap increase to 125

## **Getting There**

You can get to Laurion's Song zones through several ways.

- Via the Guild Hall portal NPC [Zeflmin Werlikanin](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=17516). Purchase Medal of Heroes from him and give it to him to be ported to Laurion Inn near the Plane of Tranquility door at /way -357, -1270, -12.
- You can get to Laurion Inn through Plane of Tranquility at /way -738, -800, -911. This spot is on the FIND tool via CTRL F.
- Druid and Wizard ports will take you to Laurion Inn as well.
