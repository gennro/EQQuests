## **Introduction**

Terror of Luclin is EverQuest's 28th expansion. It launched December 7th, 2021.

* * *

## **Content & Features**

- 7 Expansion Zones
- New Raids, Quests, and Missions
- New Spells, Combat Abilities, and AAs
- New Collections
- Level increase to 120
- Teleport Item Key Ring - Every character on your account will get a 10-slot Key Ring to store teleportation items! You can add slots with marketplace items as you need them!
- New Weapon Ornaments

## **Getting There**

You can get to Terror of Luclin zones through several ways.

The natural way is through The Nexus, on the south side (Kunark portal) is [a concerned shade](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=57337). Speak to it for a port stone and then stand in the middle of the Nexus to be ported to Maiden's Eye at /loc 618, 1297, -113 right behind Senshali Sa Sivuela in the nortwest part of the zone.

Druids and Wizards can port to Shadow Valley, the port spells are bought on merchants in the southeast corner of Maiden's Eye behind the fake wall behind [Horfitz Dappledink](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=57277).

The guild hall port vendor sells Gem of the Maiden's Tempest for 472.499pp that ports to /loc -971, -1273, -106 in southeast Maiden's Eye right near the quest giver Diablo Sivuela.

You say, 'I don't know anything about what is happening. It sounds like Methinon invited a bunch of Barbarians to invade this land. The first invasion went poorly, and the subsequent rescue missions have also failed.'
Daerius Jaegir says, 'Those Barbarians aren't the most [forthcoming].'
You say, 'I have found most of them to be less than forthcoming. But Istoph Banock [npc=56955] did mention that she had family that went missing.'
Daerius Jaegir says, 'Banock. That name sounds familiar. I have heard that name before. You should look to the Basilica for more information on that family. I digress. The samples that you have brought me, from the creatures and the fulgurites, seem to be [toxic].'
You say, 'Toxic? I have been carrying those around!'
Daerius Jaegir says, 'Yes, they have an interesting effect. The shadows mixed with this power. It [really want]s to keep its secret.'
You say, 'How can rocks really want anything?'
Daerius Jaegir says, 'Look where you stand. This land belongs to one goddess and she holds [dominion] over all you see.'
You say, 'She does not have dominion over me.'
Daerius Jaegir says, 'Indeed. And perhaps over some of the other creatures here. There are shadow wolves [npc=56933] to the west of here, that may also have been altered. I believe these unfortunate creatures were lost in this land and changed by their experiences here. Bring me any clues of their alteration and we may get to the bottom of this mystery.'

You say, 'I have come across grim shades and drifting shadows.'
