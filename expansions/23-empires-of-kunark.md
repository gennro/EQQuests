### **Introduction**

**Empires of Kunark** is EverQuest's 23rd expansion. It will launch on Wednesday, November 16, 2016

* * *

### **Content & Features**

- 7 Expansion Zones
- Familiar Key Ring
- New Quests, Heroic Adventures, Missions, and Additional Raids
- [New Spells](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/wiki/eq:Empires_of_Kunark_Spells) and AAs
- New AA cap - The stored AA cap is now 2x your current level.

## **Getting There**

Lceanium is the base zone for the expansion. In order to get to Lceanium, visit The Nexus in the Luclin expansion. NPC's at the center of The Nexus give out port stones. Put one in your bag and wait to be ported up.

## **Progression**

- Progression through Empires of Kunark is strictly linear with a story line that spans 68 quests through all 7 zones. Tiers 1 & 2 zones are unlocked as you make your way through the expansion story line.
