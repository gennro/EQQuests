## **Introduction**

The Outer Brood is EverQuest's 31st expansion. It will launch in December 2024.

* * *

## **Content & Features**

- 6 Expansion Zones
- New Raids, Quests, and Missions
- New Spells, Combat Abilities, and AAs
- New Collections
- New race and class combination: Dark Elf Ranger - For the first time, Dark Elves can be Rangers! Join the hunt and mercilessly stalk your prey as a Dark Elf Ranger. DE Rangers will be available on progression servers as well.
- New Weapon Ornaments
- Activated Items Key Ring - A new key ring that accepts most nonexpendable items with activatable effects that are not already part of another key ring.

## **Getting There**

You can get to The Outer Brood zones through several ways.

- Via the Guild Hall portal NPC [Zeflmin Werlikanin](https://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=17516). Purchase insert Broken Auretite Chain from him and give it to him to be ported to Hodstock Hills at some /way 1228, 2176, 466
- You can get to Hodstock Hills through Nektulos Forest at /way -1019, 1010, 5. Click the gate at the east end of the river. This spot is on the FIND tool via CTRL F.
- You can also get to Hodstock Hills through East Freeport at /way 663, -1453, 9 west of Bait Masterson. Click the door at the north part of the docks along the north wall. This spot is on the FIND tool via CTRL F.
- Druid and Wizard ports will take you to Hodstock Hills as well.
