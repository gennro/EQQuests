## **Introduction**

Claws of Veeshan is EverQuest's 27th expansion. It launched December 8th, 2020.

* * *

## **Content & Features**

- 6 Expansion Zones
- New Raids, Quests, and Missions
- New Spells/Tomes, and AAs
- New Collections
- Dragons Hoard ( [Source](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq/index.php?threads/test-update-12-01-2020-patch-notes-and-discussions.271951/)) \- Requires owning Claws of Veeshan. You start with 100 slots if your account is all access.

  - The Dragon's Hoard is now avaliable. To find it, open the bank and click on the Dragon's Hoard button.

    - This will act as a separate bank for each character and allow you to store additional items.
    - It can be accessed from any banker in the world.
    - This gives you the option to store your items in a list based format that is easily sorted and auto-merges your stacks of items for you.
    - The cap for each character's Hoard is 100 items for members, 25 items for non-members, and 100 additional items from purchased Dragon's Hoard Slots.
    - Dragon's Hoard items will only show up in Find Item window searches when the Dragon's Hoard window is open.
    - Dragon's Hoard items will be listed in /outputfile inventory if it is run when the Dragon's Hoard window is open.
    - You can purchase extra slots from the Daybreak Marketplace.

      - 5 slots for 399 DB Cash
      - 10 slots for 699 DB Cash
      - 25 slots for 1499 DB Cash

- New Weapon Ornaments

## **Getting There**

You can get to Claws of Veeshan zones through several ways.

Sleeper's Tomb can be accessed through Eastern Wastes.

Cobalt Scar can be accessed through Kael Drakkel(ToV).

Druids and Wizards can port to Cobalt Scar, the port spells are bought at the druid ring in Cobalt Scar.

The guild hall port vendor sells Othmir Clamshell for 446.249pp that ports to /loc -864, -456, 80 on the south beach of Cobalt Scar.

**Task Window Text:** Provisioner Tarka is in need of some seashells that are located in the Cobalt Scar, amongst the restless ice. Search the shorelines for small seashells. Sirens and Neriads collect medium seashells, presumably for fashion. Large seashells are collected by the ulthorks and used as tools typically, but because they have been most taken by the ice, they have no need for the shells.
